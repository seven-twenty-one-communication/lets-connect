## Evidence and decision rules

Public listing claims do not establish exact screens, workflows, pricing or provider implementation. The user-provided product brief is a requested target, not proof of reference behavior. Unknowns remain decisions; monetization, location, KYC document collection and recording must not be invented or activated without approval and applicable review.
